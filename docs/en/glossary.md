# Glossary of Terms

| English Term | Arabic Equivalent | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Tenant | مستأجر / منظمة عميل | Top-level customer boundary representing an independent enterprise or corporate group. |
| Company | شركة / كيان قانوني | Registered legal entity belonging to a Tenant with its own tax identification, currency, and financial books. |
| Branch | فرع / موقع تشغيلي | Physical site, office, store, or warehouse belonging to a Company. |
| Membership | عضوية | Explicit association of a User with a Tenant, defining scoped access and roles. |
| Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) | التحكم بالوصول المستند إلى الأدوار | Access model where permissions are grouped into roles and assigned to memberships. |
| Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) | التحقق متعدد العوامل | Security mechanism requiring two or more verification factors before granting access. |
| Audit Log | سجل التدقيق | Tamper-evident, immutable event log tracking who performed which action on what entity and when. |
| Party | طرف تجاري | Unified entity representing a customer, vendor, or business contact. |
| General Ledger | دفتر الأستاذ العام | The master collection of all balanced financial accounts and posted journal transactions. |
| Journal Entry | قيد يومية | Balanced financial record consisting of debits and credits in the company's functional currency. |
